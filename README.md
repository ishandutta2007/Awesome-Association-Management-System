# Awesome-Association-Management-System

## Top Association Management System (AMS) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Membership Management, Member Engagement & Nonprofit Operations*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Association Management Systems (AMS)**. These tools manage membership databases, dues and renewals, event registration, communications, and member engagement for associations, chambers of commerce, nonprofits, and clubs.

**Examples** include Nimble AMS, Higher Logic Thrive, WildApricot, YourMembership, MemberClicks, GrowthZone, Glue Up, MemberSuite, iMIS, Fonteva, Personify360, and CiviCRM (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom membership workflows, and transparent nonprofit data management — ideal for associations, clubs, NGOs, and developers building vendor-independent AMS solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Nimble AMS](https://www.nimbleams.com/)**  
  Association management system built natively on Salesforce, offering AI, predictive analytics, and automation for member engagement.

- **[Higher Logic Thrive](https://www.higherlogic.com/)**  
  All-in-one community and marketing platform for associations, featuring AI-powered member support and AMS integrations.

- **[WildApricot](https://www.wildapricot.com/)**  
  All-in-one membership management software for small associations, nonprofits, and clubs with website builder and payment processing.

- **[YourMembership](https://www.yourmembership.com/)**  
  All-in-one AMS with member portal, online community, event management, and e-commerce capabilities.

- **[MemberClicks](https://memberclicks.com/)**  
  Membership management platform for associations and chambers, with MC Professional and MC Trade tiers for different organizational complexities.

- **[GrowthZone](https://www.growthzone.com/)**  
  Comprehensive AMS ecosystem covering member management, events, billing, marketing automation, and website portals.

- **[Glue Up](https://www.glueup.com/)**  
  Engagement management platform with events, membership, CPD credits tracking, and community features.

- **[MemberSuite](https://www.membersuite.com/)**  
  Cloud-based AMS designed for associations with complex membership structures and organizational members.

- **[iMIS](https://www.imis.com/)**  
  Long-standing association management platform from ASI, offering member data management, billing, events, and website tools.

- **[Fonteva](https://www.fonteva.com/)**  
  Events and membership management solution built 100% native to Salesforce.

- **[Personify360](https://www.personifycorp.com/)**  
  Enterprise-level AMS with deep integration capabilities, including Outlook plugin for contact tracking.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  
  The leading open-source CRM for nonprofits and associations. Comprehensive constituent management, donations, events, memberships, and email campaigns. Integrates with WordPress, Drupal, Joomla, and Backdrop. 

- **[Tendenci](https://github.com/tendenci/tendenci)**  
  Open-source association management system and CMS built for nonprofits. Features membership management, event registration, donations, forums, and photo galleries. Licensed under GNU GPLv3+. 

- **[Galette](https://github.com/galette/galette)**  
  Membership management web application for nonprofits and associations, released under GPLv3. Actively maintained since 2013, with a focus on French and European associations. 

- **[Hitobito](https://github.com/hitobito/hitobito)**  
  Open-source web application for managing complex group hierarchies with members, events, courses, and mailings. Plugin architecture via Wagons for custom organizational structures. Used by Swiss youth organizations and associations. 

- **[Byro](https://github.com/byro/byro)**  
  Plugin-based, unopinionated membership administration software for small and medium clubs, NGOs, and associations. Focused on the DACH region with Mailman integration and German nonprofit receipt tools. 

- **[AMS (DTTA)](https://github.com/digital-technologies-teachers-aotearoa/ams)**  
  Modern open-source association management platform built with Django and Wagtail. Features individual/organization memberships, Xero accounting integration, Discourse forum SSO, and bilingual support. 

- **[Open-Club-Manager](https://github.com/Code-Institute-Submissions/Open-Club-Manager)**  
  Django-based club management system with user authentication, booking management, custom theming, and Stripe payment integration. Designed for sports clubs and small organizations. 

- **[Narvik](https://github.com/Narvik-app/frontend)**  
  SaaS-style association management solution released under GNU AGPLv3. Features member management, seasons, point-of-sale module, email templates, and multi-role support (Admin, Member, Badgeuse). 

- **[CJ-7](https://github.com/NEJANX/CJ-7)**  
  Web-based member management system for clubs and societies, built in PHP. Suitable for schools and organizations needing a lightweight membership solution. 

- **[MMM (Modular Membership Management)](https://git.kabelsalat.ch/mmm/mmm)**  
  Python/Flask membership management webservice released under the MIT License. Designed for association membership management with modular architecture. 

- **[Sinx](https://sinx.intervista.org)**  
  Italian management application for nonprofit associations, released under GPL. Features member registry, accounting, activity management, forms, and calendar events. Written in PHP with MySQL. 

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Chinese Language Society Portal** — PHP-based society management with member registration, event bookings, ticketing, and admin dashboard. 
- **Pino** — Drupal 8 distribution for managing members and their metadata, open source. 
- **NABIP Association Management** — Modern association management template with chapter analytics, role-based access control, and financial tracking. 

**Frameworks for building custom AMS solutions**: Combine **CiviCRM** with **WordPress** or **Drupal** for content management, **Hitobito** with custom Wagons plugins for complex hierarchies, or **Galette** for lightweight French/European association needs. For modern Python stacks, consider **DTTA AMS** (Django + Wagtail) or **Narvik** (Vue + TypeScript).
